# Cross-check: Occultist Explorer's 72 spirits against the knowledge base

Date: 2026-11-10. The app's data is in `spirits.json`, extracted from the owner's `engine/spirits.js`.

## Witnesses compared, oldest first

| Witness | Date | How it was read |
|---|---|---|
| Weyer, *Pseudomonarchia daemonum* (Latin; Scot's English 1584) | 1577 | Peterson's esotericarchives edition (`weyer.htm`). The Latin clauses for every disputed count were checked word for word |
| Sloane 3825, Goetia | 1641+ | our render notes of the manuscript (`render/sloane3825/notes/goetia.txt`) |
| Goetia, 1904 edition | 1904 | Gutenberg 72679 |
| The app (de Laurence 1916, a reprint of 1904) | 1916 | `spirits.json` |

Oberon (c. 1577-83) and Wellcome 110 were not checked: no transcription is to hand.

**The rule applied** (`authority.md` §4.1, `goetia` rite): names, offices, legions and directions come from the oldest *Officium* witness, here Weyer. Where Weyer is silent or lacks the spirit, Sloane 3825 decides; where both are silent, 1904.

## Result

- **Order and numbers:** all 72 are present and in the right order. Weyer lacks Vassago (3), Seere (70), Dantalion (71) and Andromalius (72); Sloane 3825 decides for those.
- **Office quotes:** every one of the app's office and malice quotes matches the 1904 text. Asmoday's adds a line from the Testament of Solomon, which the app attributes. But the app keeps **one office per spirit**, and the sources give most spirits several (Furfur also raises "Great Tempestuous Storms"). The offices were not compared line by line with Weyer's Latin.

## The question this raises

The ruling of 4.1 names names, offices, legions and directions, but **not rank**. Rank matters because it decides the seal metal, and the metal table is Sloane 3825's own. So there are two readings:

1. **Rank from Sloane 3825.** It is the same work as the metal table and the seals, so seal, rank and metal all come from one book. Weyer's ranks are recorded as variants.
2. **Rank from Weyer**, like legions. Decarabia would then be a king and earl, and his seal gold or copper-and-silver, although Sloane 3825 draws and classes him as a marquis.

**Ruling (the owner, 2026-11-10): reading 1.** Rank comes from Sloane 3825; Weyer's ranks are recorded as variants. Applied to the six spirits:
